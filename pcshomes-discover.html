North Country Living · Life around Fort Drum

“Is Fort Drum really that bad?”

We get it — nobody Googles Fort Drum and gets excited. But the families who actually live here? Most don't want to leave. Beaches 20 minutes out, castles on islands, the snowiest hills east of the Rockies, and a festival calendar that runs all twelve months. Here's the whole brief.

With kids, week to week

What actually fills a Tuesday in January

Honest answer: yes, this is a good place to raise kids. The community is tight, the outdoor access is unreal, and the week-to-week routine is more full than the Google results suggest.

Indoor play — winter-critical
Billy Beez — Salmon Run MallThe big indoor play park, and effectively the indoor playground now — the mall's other play spots have closed. Plan on it all winter. (315) 816-4515
Pine Plains Bowling Center — on post24 lanes with bumpers, HyperBowling, snack bar, birthday parties. Closed Mon–Tue. (315) 772-6601
Watertown Family YMCAGymnastics from 18 months (Parent & Tot, Tumbling Tigers, Ninja classes) at the Fairgrounds and Carthage sites — around $38/mo for members.
CYSS + SKIES Unlimited — on postDance, piano, gymnastics, martial arts, STEM/robotics via WebTrac. Register with Parent Central the day you get orders — the childcare waitlist runs 3–6 months. (315) 772-8675
Storytimes — one at nearly every library
Carthage Free LibraryStory Time Wednesdays 10:30–11:45am, ages 0–4 — stories, craft, music. Summer reading for K–6.
Flower Memorial Library, WatertownChildren's department with playrooms, puppet theater, costume area. Preschool story programs — call Children's for the current schedule: (315) 785-7709
Hay Memorial Library, Sackets HarborMorning storytimes plus interactive Story Walks. (315) 646-2228
Evans Mills Public Library & Bodman Memorial, PhiladelphiaPreschool story programs and summer reading closest to the gate-side villages.
Swim & splash
Magrath indoor pool — on post25m pool with "Learn to Swim" family and private lessons — free for active-duty soldiers. (315) 772-4806
Watertown Family YMCALessons from 6 months to adult at the downtown aquatic center — Saturday blocks for Parent & Tot through Level 6, plus Carthage-site lessons.
City poolsThompson Park Pool and the Alteri Pool at the Duffy Fairgrounds. (315) 785-7775
Splash padsThompson Park and North Elementary (Flynn) — open mid-May, roughly 10am–8pm daily in season. Free.
Worth the trip, every time
Zoo New York at Thompson ParkThe only zoo in the world devoted to New York–native species — bears, wolves, wolverines, eagles, mountain lions. May–Oct daily 10–5; April weekends 10–5; Nov–Mar weekends 11–3 plus school breaks. A 2025 funding crisis nearly closed it — go, your admission genuinely matters. (315) 782-6180
Old McDonald's Farm — Sackets Harbor200+ animals, storybook hayride, mini golf, bounce pad; corn maze and pumpkin patch in fall. $15.95 ages 2+, closed Mon–Tue.
Sackets Harbor Battlefield, with kidsLiving-history reenactors in 1813 uniform all summer, a flat year-round interpretive trail, and picnic tables over Lake Ontario. Free grounds — the easiest history lesson you'll ever run.

Bigger days out: Inman Gulf's Rainbow Falls loop (6.9 mi over a 200–300-ft canyon), Gleasmans Falls (6.6 mi round trip), and Robert G. Wehle State Park's Lake Ontario bluffs — famous sunsets, but keep kids off the edges. On post: the Fort Drum Trails and James R. Kanik Nature Trail.

Beaches — summer
Westcott Beach State ParkThe closest real beach — ~20 min, $7/car, lifeguarded swimming, picnic tables, minutes from Sackets Harbor.
Southwick Beach State Park3,500 feet of sand — among the best on Lake Ontario. ~40 min, $8/car May–Oct; guarded swimming June 19–Sept 6 in 2026.
Remington Park — on postBeach on Remington Pond, May–Oct, plus pavilions, playgrounds, fishing docks, and cabin/RV rentals. (315) 772-8222
Wellesley Island State ParkBeach at the largest camping complex in the Thousand Islands — pair it with the nature center.
Fishing — year-round
Henderson Harbor — the "Golden Crescent"60+ Lake Ontario charters: lake trout, king salmon, browns, steelhead, smallmouth. Season runs roughly April–September.
Black RiverBass and pike water 10 minutes from post, plus fall salmon and steelhead runs. Charters from around $120.
Salmon River, Pulaski (~50 min)The most famous Lake Ontario tributary — 15–40-lb king salmon Sept–Oct, steelhead fall through spring.
Ice fishingRent everything from MWR Outdoor Rec's Gear to Go — see winter, below.
Winter — the Tug Hill snow machine
Dry Hill Ski Area10 min south of downtown Watertown — 7 trails, 300-ft vertical, terrain park, snow tubing with snowmaking. The learn-to-ski hill, with military discounts. (315) 782-8584
Snow Ridge, Turin (~50 min)230″ average snowfall — 22 trails, 6 lifts, night skiing, glades, tubing, backcountry acreage.
Maple Ridge Snow Park, LowvilleThe longest tubing lanes in the North Country — 1,200 ft with a tube tow, weekends and school holidays 12–5, natural snow only. Operations have changed hands — verify it's running each season before you drive.
MWR Gear to Go — on postRent ski/snowboard packages, snowshoes, XC skis, fat-tire bikes, and ice-fishing gear cheap; boats, campers, and canoes in summer. (315) 772-8222
Snowmobiling on Tug HillClub-groomed trail networks: Osceola Snowmobile Club, Penn Mountain Snow Riders, Turin Ridge Riders, Valley Snow Travelers. Trail info at tughill.org.
Sledding, free versionThompson Park's famous hill — or Dry Hill's tubing park for the lift-served upgrade. XC ski and snowshoe at Whetstone Gulf, Wellesley Island, and Wehle.

Crossing into Canada: passport, passport card, or NEXUS required at the Thousand Islands Bridge (toll both ways). Service members — confirm the current leave/pass policy for international travel with your chain of command; Fort Drum has an on-post Official Passport and Visa Center.

The year, month by month

There is always something on the calendar
Jan – FebSnowtown USA at Thompson Park — skating at the rotary pavilion; the "Snowtown" name goes back to Walter Cronkite's Blizzard of '77 coverage. · Ottawa's Winterlude, Jan 30–Feb 16 in 2026 — ice sculptures, a snow playground, mostly free.
MarchNYS Maple Weekend — sugarhouses across Lewis and Jefferson counties open their doors over two weekends; the Maple Museum in Croghan runs pancake breakfasts ($12 adult / $7 child).
MayBoldt Castle opens for the season, mid-May.
JuneBeaches open · Singer Castle shuttle season starts (June 17 in 2026) · Mountainfest on post — free festival with kids' games, military demos, and a free headline concert (2026: Nate Smith and Chayce Beckham).
JulyCape Vincent French Festival, July 10–11 in 2026 (56th year) — pastries, parade, fireworks · Jefferson County Fair, July 14–19, 2026 — the oldest continuously running fair in the US, since 1817 (per jeffcofair.org) · Can-Am Festival in Sackets Harbor — parade, fireworks, vintage baseball, soapbox derby; confirm dates with the village.
AugustAntique Boat Show & Auction in Clayton — America's oldest, first weekend · Bill Johnston's Pirate Days, Alexandria Bay, Aug 14–16 in 2026 — pirate invasion Saturday, downtown parade Sunday.
Sept – OctThe salmon run hits the Salmon River (kings 15–40 lb) · Burrville cider season peaks with the foliage · Boo at the Zoo — Halloween-season weekends 10–3, costumed trick-or-treating.
Nov – MarWatertown Wolves hockey at the Municipal Arena — family-priced pro hockey with frequent military appreciation nights. (Watertown Rapids collegiate baseball takes the summer shift.)
DecemberWatertown Christmas Parade — Washington Street to City Hall, free hot chocolate from the downtown businesses.

The discount stack

Bring your ID everywhere
LTS/ITR office — on post, first stopMon–Fri 8:00–2:30 · (315) 772-8222 or (315) 772-5169. Current price list examples: Disney World 4–6 day Park Hoppers $409–$479, Disneyland $314–$505, Universal Orlando $215–$255, Legoland NY from $49.50 — all under gate price. Call for the full list.
Singer CastlePublished military rate: $12.75 adult / $6.75 child (vs $14.75 / $7.75).
Dry Hill Ski AreaMilitary discounts on skiing and tubing — and it's the cheap local hill to begin with.
Watertown WolvesRecurring military appreciation nights — discounted admission, color guard, re-enlistment ceremonies on the ice.
New York State Fair, SyracuseFree-admission military day every year (fair runs late Aug–Labor Day).
HotelsTripAdvisor keeps a running list of Watertown hotels with military rates — useful for visiting family.

Water Safari, Uncle Sam Boat Tours, and Boldt Castle discounts are commonly reported but not published — ask at the window with your ID before you pay full price.
