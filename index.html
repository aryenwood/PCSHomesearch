Local intel

Things nobody tells you

The stuff you only learn after living here
Natural gas vs. propane = $1,500/yearWatertown city has gas lines; Evans Mills and most rural areas run propane or oil. We know which streets have gas.
Same street, different school districtFort Drum spans 4 districts and the boundary lines are confusing. Confirm the district before you offer — not after.
Register for CYSS childcare the day you get ordersThe waitlist runs 3–6 months. Sign up at militarychildcare.com before you arrive.
NY spouses get automatic license reciprocityNursing, teaching, real estate, and 10+ professions — with a 6-month temporary permit while your transfer processes.
Veterans get 3 NY property tax exemptions — most miss 2458-a, 458-b, and Eligible Funds. Combined with STAR, that's $1,000+/year many families leave on the table.
Snow tires aren't optional140+ inches a year. Budget $400–800 for a set, and strongly consider AWD. Studded tires are legal Oct 16 – Apr 30.
