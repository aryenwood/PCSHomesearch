    What Joe tells families moving in.

    Joseph Murtha grew up here and sells homes around Fort Drum. Three short videos.

    Ask for the seller's rate

    A VA loan can be taken over by the next buyer, and some sellers here have rates from 2.5% to 5%. It's rarely advertised, so ask. Expect some money up front and extra steps.

    Watch on TikTok →

    Pull a year of heating bills

    Buying or renting, ask for the last 12 months of utility bills and look hard at December through March. BAH against rent or mortgage isn't the whole picture.

    Watch on TikTok →

    Don't skip the duplex

    Live in one side and rent the other to keep your monthly costs low. Joe bought one in his early 20s and still owns it.

    Watch on TikTok →
