Local intel

Things nobody tells you.

The stuff you only learn after living here.

01

Natural gas vs. propane = $1,500/year

Watertown city has gas lines; Evans Mills and most rural areas run propane or oil. We know which streets have gas.

02

Same street, different school district

Fort Drum spans 4 districts and the boundary lines are confusing. Confirm the district before you offer — not after.

03

Register for CYSS childcare the day you get orders

The waitlist runs 3–6 months. Sign up at militarychildcare.com before you arrive.

04

NY fast-tracks military-spouse licenses

Expedited review, a halved application fee, and a supervised 6-month temporary permit in eligible professions like nursing, teaching, and real estate. Not automatic — start the paperwork early.

05

NY has 3 veteran property-tax exemptions — you can claim one

Alternative (458-a) is usually the biggest; Cold War (458-b) and Eligible Funds are the others. Pick the one that fits, stack it with STAR, and file by March 1 — none of it happens automatically.

06

Snow tires aren't optional

140+ inches a year. Budget $400–800 for a set, and strongly consider AWD. Studded tires are legal Oct 16 – Apr 30.
