Living here · Fort Drum

Is Fort Drum really that bad?

That's Boldt Castle, on an island in the St. Lawrence, about 44 minutes from post. There's a Lake Ontario beach about 34 minutes away, a free city park with a zoo next door, and 104 family places below, closest first.

18minA free city park with a splash pad, a pool, the sledding hill and Zoo New York next door 29minLake Ontario sunsets and a War of 1812 battlefield in Sackets Harbor 44min1,864 islands, two countries, and Boldt Castle by boat

Your first free Saturday

Three free places to go before the boxes are unpacked.

Thompson Park

An Olmsted-designed city park: playground, splash pad, pool, paved paths, and Zoo New York on the grounds.

Sackets Harbor

A War of 1812 battlefield on Lake Ontario, a walkable village, and kids 12 and under free at the historic site.

Black River Trail

4.5 flat, paved miles along the river: bikes, strollers, and a picnic at the end.

Lake days, every weekend

  • Westcott Beach swim season, June 19 to Sept 6, 2026

Cider, corn mazes and color

  • Burrville Cider Mill donuts, Labor Day to Thanksgiving

Licenses at the resident price

Stationed in New York more than 30 days? You pay the resident price for fishing, hunting and trapping licenses. Fishing takes a license from age 16, except on free fishing days: Feb. 14–15, June 27–28, Sept. 26 and Nov. 11 in 2026.

DEC: military licenses →

Hunting and fishing on post

The training areas are open for recreation with a Fort Drum Recreational Access Pass ($25 for adults 18 and older, good through Dec. 31) and your New York license. Check in before you go, online or at 1-855-267-9770.

Fort Drum iSportsman →

Your first New York hunt

First-time hunters take hunter education before their first license, and bowhunters after deer or bear take a bowhunter course too. New York accepts hunter education certificates from other states, and both courses run online.

DEC: hunter education →

Snowmobiles

A New York snowmobile registration costs $65 with a snowmobile club voucher and $135 without. The snowmobile clubs below groom the trails.
