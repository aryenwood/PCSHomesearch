Good to know before you arrive.

  • Meet your unit's families

    Your unit's Soldier and Family Readiness Group (SFRG, once called the FRG) is the quickest way to meet other families and get unit news. Your sponsor can share the leader's contact before you arrive.

  • Spouse careers

    Tuition money, license reimbursement, New York license transfers and remote-work leads are in the Spouse HQ.

  • School vaccines

    New York allows only medical exemptions from school vaccination requirements; the religious exemption ended in 2019. Bring shot records when you enroll.

  • Deploying soon after the move?

    Connect with your SFRG leader early, register for child care right away, and make sure Army Community Service has your contact information. You are not alone in this.

Getting around.

  • You'll need a car

    Towns are spread out and winters are long; see Winter for what to set up. Drive times from 30 towns to the gates are in Housing.

  • Flying in and out

    Watertown International (ART) is in the area, with American Airlines flights to Philadelphia. Syracuse Hancock (SYR) is about 90 minutes south on I-81, with Allegiant, American, Breeze, Delta, Frontier, JetBlue, Sun Country and United.

  • Weekends away

    Ottawa is about two hours north and Montreal about three. The Thousand Islands and the Adirondacks are closer: see Living Here.

  • Internet and phone

    Check the exact address with internet providers before you sign; some rural addresses only get satellite. If your phone struggles indoors, turn on Wi-Fi calling, and ask your carrier about a military discount.
