Chapter · Healthcare at Fort Drum

There's no hospital on post. Know where to go.

Fort Drum has a clinic, not a hospital. Emergencies, hospital stays and births happen in Watertown. Save these four numbers before you arrive.

Where you'll get care.

In an emergency, call 911. For everything else, this is who does what.

Guthrie Army Health Clinic

On post at 11050 Mount Belvedere Blvd. An outpatient clinic: primary care and specialty clinics, with no emergency room, no hospital beds and no labor and delivery. Soldiers get care here, and family members can be assigned a primary care doctor here or with a civilian doctor in the TRICARE network.

1-888-838-1303Appointments

Samaritan Medical Center

Watertown's hospital, at 830 Washington St. The emergency room, hospital stays, maternity care, and a provisional Level III adult trauma center (designated by New York State in 2024). It accepts TRICARE. The region's Level I trauma center, for adults and children, is Upstate University Hospital in Syracuse.

(315) 785-4000Main line

TRICARE, in plain words.

TRICARE is the military's health plan. Fort Drum is in the East Region, run by Humana Military.

  • Enroll when you arrive

    Call TRICARE East at 1-800-444-5445 or update your enrollment at tricare.mil so your coverage and your primary care doctor move to Fort Drum.

  • Prime or Select

    Prime gives you a primary care manager, at Guthrie or a civilian network doctor, who sends you to specialists with a referral. Select lets you see any TRICARE-authorized provider without a referral, but you pay a yearly deductible and a share of each visit.

  • Not sure if it can wait?

    The Nurse Advice Line answers 24/7 at 1-800-874-2273 (press 1) and tells you whether to go to urgent care, the emergency room, or book an appointment.

  • Dental is separate

    Soldiers go to the dental clinic on post. Family members use the TRICARE Dental Program, run by United Concordia: enroll through TRICARE.

Coverage and referral rules change. Check your plan at tricare.mil before you book non-emergency care.

PCSing while pregnant.

Three calls to make in your first week, so your care picks up where it left off.

01

Book with OB-GYN

Guthrie Army Health Clinic books OB-GYN appointments at 1-888-838-1303 or (315) 844-0220. Bring your records from your last doctor.

02

Know where you'll deliver

Samaritan Medical Center's Car-Freshner Center for Women and Children in Watertown, home to the only Level II NICU north of Syracuse. Maternity: (315) 785-4050.

03

Sign up for WIC

Food and nutrition help for pregnant women and young kids. The Jefferson County WIC office is at 238 Arsenal St, Watertown: (315) 782-9222.

Someone to talk to.

  • Military OneSource

    Free, confidential counseling for soldiers and families, 24 hours a day, by phone, video or in person: 1-800-342-9647 or militaryonesource.mil.

  • In crisis right now

    Call 988 and press 1 for the Military and Veterans Crisis Line, 24/7. Every crisis line near Fort Drum is in the directory's Help now section.
