The four districts, honestly.

Military-connected enrollment tells you how used to PCS families a district is. All four accept remote pre-enrollment. For test scores and school-by-school data, each district's official NYSED report card is the fair comparison.

General Brown CSD

~40%military

Dexter, Brownville, Glen Park

Registrar Michele Groff · (315) 779-2312

MFLC counselor on staff · forms online, call to start
Documents, enrollment steps, care & contacts

About 40% military-connected enrollment, the highest share of any off-post district here. Staff are accustomed to PCS transitions, and a Military & Family Life Counselor (MFLC) is assigned to the schools. gblions.org

Documents to bring

  1. Proof of age (birth certificate)
  2. Proof of residency (lease, utility bill, or PCS orders)
  3. Immunization records (NYS-compliant)
  4. Last report card or transcript (if transferring)
  5. IEP or 504 Plan (if applicable)
  6. Custody documentation (if applicable)

How to enroll

  1. Download registration forms from gblions.org/central-registration; you can fill them out before you arrive.
  2. Contact Registrar Michele Groff at (315) 779-2312 or mgroff@gblions.org. Tell her you're a military family PCSing in.
  3. Pre-enroll remotely under MIC3, using PCS orders as proof of residency.
  4. Finish in person at Central Registration with your original documents once you arrive.
  5. Busing is arranged at registration for all students in the district.

Mid-year, IEP, and meals

General Brown is experienced with mid-year enrollment, and the MFLC program helps military kids adjust. Bring your child's current IEP: the district must provide comparable services immediately under MIC3, then build a new plan with you. The district participates in Free and Reduced Price Lunch; the annual application is at school offices or on the district site.

Before and after school

Watertown Family YMCA runs SACC (School Age Child Care) at Brownville Elementary for General Brown families: (315) 755-2005 or sacc@watertownymca.org. Scholarships available.

The Fort Drum EFMP office and the School Liaison can coordinate before you arrive. Menus and free/reduced meal applications are on ircsd.org.

  4. Proof of immunizations (NYS-compliant)

How to enroll

  5. Bus stops, times, and routes arrive through ParentSquare starting late August.

Mid-year, IEP, and meals

Carthage accommodates mid-year enrollment for military families. For an IEP or a possible special education need, call Pupil Services at (315) 493-5067; comparable IEP services are required at enrollment under MIC3. Carthage participates in the Community Eligibility Provision: every student eats breakfast and lunch at no cost, no application needed.

Six rights the law already gave your kids.

New York adopted the Interstate Compact on Educational Opportunity for Military Children (MIC3) in 2014. These are not favors to negotiate. They are rules the district follows.

01

Enroll them now, before you move.

Districts must allow preliminary enrollment without charge using PCS orders as proof of residency, even before you arrive.

02

They keep their AP and honors seats.

If your child was in AP, honors, or gifted programs, the new school must honor that placement if an equivalent exists. No repeating coursework.

03

The IEP follows them, day one.

Schools must provide comparable services from your child's existing IEP immediately, then work with you to develop a new one.

04

They can still make the team.

Eligibility for extracurricular activities and sports tryouts cannot be denied because of transfer timing or enrollment dates.

05

They graduate on time.

Principals can accept equivalent courses from your prior school in place of New York requirements, so your child graduates with their peers.

06

No waiting on paperwork.

Unofficial records must be accepted for enrollment. Sending schools must process official records within 10 business days of the request.

If a school pushes backOne email to the New York State Compact Commissioner usually settles it: MilitaryCompact@nysed.gov

Before you move: the short list.

01

Call the School Liaison first.

Before anything else, call (315) 772-3214. They will walk you through everything.

02

Get immunization records in order early.

New York has strict requirements. Get copies before you leave your current duty station.

03

If your child has an IEP, bring three printed copies.

One for the School Liaison, one for the school, and one for you.

04

Register for CYS childcare immediately.

Waitlists fill fast. Don't wait until you arrive.

05

Join your school's Facebook group before you PCS.

Other military spouses are the best source of current, real-world information.
