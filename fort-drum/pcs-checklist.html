Know what you're owed (2026).

These entitlements are paid with your orders, but only if the numbers are right and the paperwork is filed on time. Verify everything with your finance office.

DLA (Dislocation Allowance)

One-time payment, depending on rank and dependents. Paid automatically with PCS orders. Verify with finance.

$1,018–$6,385One time

MALT (mileage)

For POV travel, based on the official distance between duty stations.

$0.205Per mile

Per diem (en route)

$110 per night for lodging plus $68 per day for meals & incidentals, for the service member; spouse at 75% of the rate, each additional dependent at 50%.

$110 + $68Lodging + meals

TLE (temporary lodging)

14 days standard (split between old and new duty station), up to 60 days in designated housing-shortage areas. Keep lodging receipts.

$290Max per day

Unaccompanied baggage

Separate from your HHG weight allowance.

2,000 lbsMax per household

Packing materials count against your weight. Overweight fees run hundreds to thousands of dollars. Weigh before you pack.

Deadlines that cost money.

01

File your travel voucher within 30 days of arrival at Fort Drum.

02

Report HHG loss/damage within 180 days of delivery, then file the itemized claim within 9 months (12 months for pickups on/after May 15, 2026), and note visible damage on the inventory before signing.

03

Photograph belongings and serial numbers before pack day. It's your evidence for claims.

04

Doing a PPM? Get certified weight tickets, empty AND loaded. Warning: PPM reimbursement rules changed. Get a cost estimate from your Transportation Office before committing.

Movers won't pack these.

  • Cleaners with bleach or ammonia, paint, gasoline & other hazmat
  • Propane tanks (even small camping ones), ammunition, fireworks
  • Plants, perishable food, and open containers
  • Medications, important documents, and jewelry: keep these with you in an essentials bag.
