On-post housing, how it works.

Fort Drum Mountain Community Homes runs on-post family housing for the Army. These rules come from the Army's Fort Drum housing FAQ.

  • Who can live on post

    Family housing is for soldiers who are married, with or without children, or single with custody of a child who lives with them. Single soldiers E-5 and below live in the barracks.

  • When to apply

    As soon as you have orders, at the Welcome Home Center, (315) 955-6644. You go active on the wait list when you sign out of your last duty station and your documents arrive. You can list the neighborhoods you prefer.

  • How the wait list is ordered

    Your rank, number of dependents and eligibility date (the day you signed out of your last duty station) set your place. Decline two offers and you go to the bottom of the list.

  • What rent covers

    Rent is your whole BAH, paid by allotment. It covers rent, utilities up to a set allowance, and renters insurance, and Mountain Community Homes handles all maintenance. For pets, see their pet policy.

  • Moving out

    Give 30 days' notice, attend a move-out briefing, and provide a copy of your orders.

  • Schools

    There's no school on post. Children living on post attend Carthage or Indian River schools, depending on the neighborhood. See the Schools chapter.

Off post, you get free help.

The Army's Housing Services Office works for you, not the landlord. Soldiers are required to check in with it before renting, leasing or buying off post.

  • What it does, free

    Housing counseling and referrals, lease reviews before you sign, safety and habitability inspections of rentals, and help settling disputes with a landlord. The Army also advises not sending a deposit before you've talked to this office.

  • Where to find it

    Clark Hall, Bldg. 10720 Mount Belvedere Blvd, Room A2-54. Weekdays 7:30 a.m. to 4 p.m. Call (315) 772-9397 or (315) 772-8751.

  • Thinking of buying?

    Run your payment against your BAH, and compare renting and buying, in the Buying chapter.

Fort Drum's gates.

  • Lt. Gen. Paul Cerjan / Main Gate

    Open 24/7, on Iraqi Freedom Drive off I-781. The Visitor Control Center (Bldg. 11008, just outside) is open 5:30 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily.

  • Col. Michael Plummer Gate

    On Mount Belvedere Blvd, near Black River. Open 4:30 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily.

  • Wheeler-Sack Army Airfield Gate

    Open 24/7. All commercial traffic uses this gate.

  • Oneida “Gasoline Alley” Gate

    Closed until further notice.

Gate hours change. Check Fort Drum's gate page before you drive in.
