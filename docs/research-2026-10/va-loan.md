# VA loan near Fort Drum: research for PCSHomes (retrieved 2026-10-27)

V = verified from a primary source I read. U = unverified. "Handbook" = VA Lenders Handbook VAP 26-7 (now at knowva.ebenefits.va.gov; the old benefits.va.gov/WARMS PDFs redirect there). Chapter update dates are as shown on the page.

## (A) Findings by topic

### 1. Eligibility and COE
- **Active duty, any branch:** 90 continuous days. V. https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/eligibility/
- **Veteran, service since Aug 2 1990:** 24 continuous months or the full period called up (min 90 days). Less for discharge due to a service-connected disability or a listed hardship/early-out exception. Older eras: 90 or 181 days. V. same page.
- **National Guard, any one of:** 90 days non-training Title 10 service; 90 days with 30 consecutive and a DD214 showing 32 USC 316/502/503/504/505 activation; 6 creditable years and still serving; 6 years plus honorable discharge or retired list. V.
- **Reserve:** 90 days non-training active duty, or 6 creditable Selected Reserve years (serving, honorably discharged or retired). V.
- **Surviving spouse:** receiving or eligible for DIC, or spouse of a service member MIA or POW. V.
- **COE documents** (https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/how-to-request-coe/, updated Sep 22 2026). V.
  - Veteran: DD214.
  - Active duty: statement of service signed by commander, adjutant or personnel officer (name, SSN, DOB, entry date, lost time, providing command).
  - Guard or Reserve ever activated: DD214. A Guard member relying on 90 days with 30 consecutive also needs a 32 USC DD214, an annual point statement, or a DD220 with orders.
  - Guard or Reserve serving, never activated: signed statement of service showing total creditable years.
  - Discharged Guard, never activated: NGB Form 22 for each period, NGB Form 23 points statement, proof of character of service.
  - Discharged Reserve, never activated: latest annual retirement points statement and proof of honorable service.
  - Surviving spouse with DIC: VA Form 26-1817. Without DIC: VA Form 21P-534EZ, marriage license, veteran's death certificate (plus DD214 if available).
- **How to request:** online form; through the lender (Web LGY); or mail VA Form 26-1880 to the regional loan center ("mail requests may take longer"). V.

### 2. Funding fee (effective Apr 7 2023; https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/funding-fee-and-closing-costs/). V.
Purchase and construction (veterans, active duty, Guard and Reserve all the same since 2023):

| Down payment | First use | Later use |
|---|---|---|
| under 5% | 2.15% | 3.3% |
| 5% or more | 1.5% | 1.5% |
| 10% or more | 1.25% | 1.25% |

- **Cash-out refinance:** 2.15% first use, 3.3% later use. **IRRRL:** 0.5%. **Assumption:** 0.5%. **Manufactured home not permanently affixed:** 1%. **Vendee:** 2.25%.
- **Exempt:** receives VA disability compensation; eligible for it but receives retirement or active-duty pay instead; surviving spouse receiving DIC; service member with a proposed or memorandum rating before closing; active duty with evidence of a Purple Heart on or before closing.
- **Refund:** if later awarded compensation with an effective date retroactive to before closing. A proposed or memorandum rating issued after closing does not qualify.
- **Table dates:** Circular 26-23-6 Change 2 (Jan 21 2025) moved the fee-table applicability date to June 9 2034. V.
- **Ignore:** benefits.va.gov/homeloans/purchaseco_loan_fee.asp is stale. It still says Guard and Reserve pay "slightly higher" fees.

### 3. Entitlement
- **Full entitlement:** no VA loan limit since Jan 1 2020; the lender still decides what you qualify for. V. https://www.benefits.va.gov/homeloans/purchaseco_loan_limits.asp
- **Partial entitlement:** over $144,000, remaining = 25% of the county conforming limit minus entitlement used and not restored. Lenders often cap a no-down loan at 4x remaining entitlement. V. Circular 26-25-10 (Dec 1 2025).
- **2026 one-unit limit:** Jefferson, Lewis and St. Lawrence are all $832,750 (25% = $208,187.50). V. https://www.fhfa.gov/document/d/cll/fullcountyloanlimitlist2026_hera-based_final_flat.pdf. For 2 to 4 units the single-unit limit still drives the calculation.
- **Restoration** (Handbook Ch.2 Topic 6, Jul 9 2026). V.
  - The property is sold and the loan paid in full, or a qualified veteran assumes the loan and substitutes entitlement.
  - Cash-out refinance of the same property.
  - One-time restoration if the loan is paid off but the home is kept. After that, any restoration needs all VA-financed property disposed of.
- **Second VA loan:** allowed with remaining entitlement. V. https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/loan-limits/
- **Assumption:** needs servicer approval, sometimes VA approval. A default on an assumed loan is charged to the original veteran's entitlement. V (Buyer's Guide Apr 2022 only). https://www.benefits.va.gov/homeloans/documents/docs/VA_Buyers_Guide.pdf

### 4. Occupancy (Handbook Ch.3 Topic 5, May 14 2024). V.
- Certify intent to live there. "Reasonable time" is within 60 days of closing. Longer only with a certified specific date tied to a specific future event, never beyond 12 months.
- **Active duty who cannot occupy:** the spouse's or a dependent child's occupancy (or intent) counts. For a child, the attorney-in-fact or guardian certifies.
- **Deployment:** deployed members are in temporary duty status and meet the rule, with or without a spouse home.
- **Also:** retirement within 12 months on a specific date; repairs that block move-in. **Never:** seasonal or investment use.
- **IRRRL:** only past occupancy is needed (the rent-it-out-after-PCS case).

### 5. Underwriting
- **Credit score:** "VA does not have a minimum credit score requirement" (Handbook Ch.4, Aug 26 2026). Lenders add overlays; the Buyer's Guide (2022) says lenders "typically" want 620. V/dated.
- **DTI:** "The standard is 41 percent or less." Above 41% needs extra review unless residual income beats the guideline by 20%. V. 38 CFR 36.4340(c)(3),(d)
- **Residual income, Northeast (NY included).** V. 38 CFR 36.4340(e), CFR 2025 ed., https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title38-vol2/xml/CFR-2025-title38-vol2-sec36-4340.xml

| Family size | Loan $79,999 and below | Loan $80,000 and above |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | $390 | $450 |
| 2 | $654 | $755 |
| 3 | $788 | $909 |
| 4 | $888 | $1,025 |
| 5 | $921 | $1,062 |
| each over 5, to 7 | +$75 | +$80 |

- **Military income** (Handbook Ch.4). V. BAH, BAS and clothing allowances count if verified on the LES. They are non-taxable, so they may be grossed up for DTI only, not residual income. Flight, hazard and combat pay are taxable and cannot be grossed up.

### 6. Costs
- **No PMI, no down payment** with full entitlement, if price does not exceed appraised value (buyer pays any gap in cash). V. va.gov purchase-loan page (Jan 7 2026); Buyer's Guide.
- **1% flat charge** by the lender, plus reasonable itemized fees: VA appraisal, recording fees and taxes, credit report, prepaids, hazard insurance, survey, title, flood determination, MERS. V. 38 CFR 36.4313(d); Handbook Ch.8 Topic 2 (Jul 9 2026).
- **Only the funding fee can be financed** on a purchase. V.
- **Seller concessions capped at 4%** of reasonable value. Counted: seller-paid funding fee, prepaid taxes or insurance, gifts, extra or buydown points, payoff of the buyer's debts. Not counted: the buyer's normal closing costs and market-rate points. V. Handbook Ch.8 Topic 5.
- **Buyer-agent fees:**
  - Circular 26-24-14 (Jun 11 2024; Change 1 Aug 5 2024; effective Aug 10 2024) is still "Valid until rescinded" on VA's circulars page. V. https://www.benefits.va.gov/homeloans/resources_circulars.asp
  - The veteran may pay reasonable and customary buyer-broker charges where listing brokers can't set buyer-broker pay through MLS or it can't flow through the listing broker. The charge is not financed, counts against liquid assets, and goes in Closing Disclosure Section H.
  - A seller paying it is not a concession. VA says a permanent rule will follow notice-and-comment rulemaking; I found none final.
  - Handbook Ch.8 (Jul 2026) still says veterans "may not, under any circumstances" pay buyer-broker fees. That conflicts with the circular, so tell families to ask the lender.
- **NY allowable fees:** VA's State Fees list (Feb 17 2026) permits Attorney Fee, CEMA Fee, Equalization Charge and 253 Affidavit. V. https://www.benefits.va.gov/homeloans/documents/docs/state-deviations.pdf

### 7. Appraisal and minimum property requirements (Handbook Ch.10, 12)
- **Process:** lender orders a VA fee appraiser after a signed contract; result is the Notice of Value (NOV). Inspection is not required but "highly recommended"; the appraisal is not an inspection. If value falls short: reconsideration (ROV), renegotiate, pay the gap, or the escape clause (earnest money back, not inspection or appraisal costs). V. Buyer's Guide.
- **Tidewater:** appraiser waits 2 business days for comparable sales (address, price, date, GLA, listing). **ROV:** written request after the NOV; VA reviews in 5 business days, or a field review within 20. V. Ch.10 Topics 8 and 22.
- **Heating** (Topic 23, changed Feb 27 2026): permanently installed, holds 50°F where there is plumbing. No fuel named. A "non-vented heater" subtopic was removed; the practical effect is U. V.
- **Wells** (Topic 16). V.
  - Water must meet the local health authority, otherwise EPA guidelines.
  - A disinterested third party collects, transports and tests the sample; the buyer never may.
  - Results are valid 90 days.
  - Dug well, cistern, spring, chlorinator or rainwater catchment needs the veteran's signed acknowledgement. So does a filtration system.
  - A shared well needs a permanent easement and a recorded sharing agreement (Topic 18).
- **Septic** (Topic 17): health-authority approval only for new construction, an appraiser-noted problem, or known percolation trouble. V.
- **Roof** (Topic 26): keeps out moisture with reasonable remaining life. **Electric** (Topic 14): frayed wires fixed. **Pre-1978 paint** (Topic 32): lead presumed; defective paint must be remediated. **Defects** (Topic 21): appraisal made "subject to" repair. V.
- **Wood-destroying insects:** VA's New York list requires WDI information only in 18 counties: Bronx, Broome, Columbia, Delaware, Dutchess, Greene, Kings, Nassau, New York, Orange, Putnam, Queens, Richmond, Rockland, Suffolk, Sullivan, Ulster, Westchester. Jefferson, Lewis and St. Lawrence are not listed, so an inspection applies only if damage is apparent. V. https://www.benefits.va.gov/HOMELOANS/appraiser_cv_local_req.asp
- **U:** county health department water-test rules for Jefferson, Lewis and St. Lawrence (VA lists none for NY).
- **Storage tanks** (Topic 35): a 1,000+ gallon flammable tank within 300 ft of the home must be reported and acknowledged. This is about neighbors' tanks, not the home's own. V.

### 8. Property types
- **2 to 4 units:** owner-occupied. Utilities may be shared only with separate shut-offs. V. va.gov purchase-loan page; Ch.12 Topic 14.
- **Condo:** the project must be VA-approved (by project, not unit). V (Ch.16 via search summary, not read in full).
- **Manufactured home:** real estate under state law, permanent foundation, HUD-code built, at least 400 sq ft single-wide or 700 double-wide. V. Ch.12 Topic 40.
- **New construction:** cost-approach appraisal available where there are no comps. **EEM:** up to $6,000 of improvements (Buyer's Guide 2022). Circular 26-25-7 (Sep 22 2025) lets a lender count a confirmed HERS-type report as a residual-income compensating factor. V.

### 9. 2025-2026 changes (official VA sources)
- **VASP ended:** Circular 26-25-2 (Apr 23 2025); no new submissions after May 1 2025, no payments after Sep 30 2025. V.
- **VA Partial Claim program** replaces it: 3-month trial plan first; servicers must be ready by Nov 28 2026. V. https://www.va.gov/resources/veterans-affairs-servicing-purchase-vasp-program-faqs/ (Sep 23 2026). The statute (P.L. 119-31) comes from a CRS report, so U.
- **Other circulars:** 26-25-1 (builder ID dropped), 26-25-7 (HERS), 26-25-10 (2026 limits), 26-26-1 (state fees), 26-24-19 Change 1 (invoices, Jun 4 2026). Handbook Ch.12 has an "effective after May 1 2026" version. V.
- **Funding fee rates unchanged** since Apr 2023. V.

### 10. New York
- **Veterans exemptions** (RPTL 458-a/458-b/458; https://www.tax.ny.gov/pdf/current_forms/orpts/rp458ai.pdf rev. 9/26; https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPT/458-A). V.
  - **Alternative (458-a):** 15% of assessed value for wartime service, +10% for combat zone or expeditionary medal, +50% of VA disability rating, each capped. Base caps $12,000 / $8,000 / $40,000 times the equalization rate; localities may lower them or raise them to $45,000 / $30,000 / $150,000 ($75,000 / $50,000 / $250,000 in high-appreciation areas).
  - **Local option:** counties, cities, towns and villages adopt by local law, school districts by resolution. School taxes are covered only where the district opted in.
  - **Who counts:** wartime includes the Persian Gulf period from Aug 2 1990 (open-ended, so all service since). Reenlisted active-duty members with 10+ years count. Reservists honorably on non-training active duty who returned to reserve status count.
  - **P&T full exemption (458-a(11)):** local option, taxable status dates on or after Oct 1 2026, same RP-458-a. Needs a VA P&T letter, 100% rating and unemployability (Chapter 672). Local adoption is U.
  - **Cold War (458-b):** service Sep 2 1945 to Dec 26 1991. Cannot combine with 458 or 458-a.
  - **Eligible funds (458):** generally up to $7,500, for property bought with pension, bonus or insurance money. It is an alternative to 458-a; where 458-a is adopted, no new 458 exemptions are granted.
  - **Filing:** with the local assessor, not the state, by taxable status date: March 1 for most towns, Jan 1 for most villages, cities per charter. Attach DD214 and deed. It continues without refiling unless the disability percentage changes.
  - **U:** which Jefferson, Lewis and St. Lawrence municipalities adopted it, and their caps.
- **Mortgage recording tax** (Form MT-15 rev. 1/25; https://www.tax.ny.gov/pdf/current_forms/mortgage/mt15.pdf). V. Jefferson and St. Lawrence: basic $0.50 + special additional $0.25 = $0.75 per $100 (a $200,000 loan = $1,500, my arithmetic). Lewis: $1.00 per $100 (adds $0.25 county tax).
  - Tax Law §253(1-a) says the special additional tax is paid by the mortgagee for six or fewer units. Who bears the basic tax is U. VA lets the veteran pay "recording fees and taxes" (38 CFR 36.4313(d)(1)(ii)). I found no VA exemption (U).
- **Attorney-led closings:** the NY attorney fee is VA-allowable (item 6). A typical closing timeline has no primary source (U); do not publish a number.

## (B) What our pages get wrong
1. **buying.html line 215:** "The VA loan is a home loan benefit for soldiers and veterans."
   - Army-centric. Correct: "service members and veterans of every branch, National Guard and Reserve members, and some surviving spouses." (va.gov/eligibility)
2. **buying.html line 217 ("No loan limit")** is right only for full entitlement and says nothing else, while the fee dropdown invites "Used a VA loan before."
   - Add: with entitlement unrestored, a county limit applies ($832,750 in all three counties in 2026; 25% less entitlement used). (Circular 26-25-10; FHFA)
3. **checklist.js "VA loan basics"** gives only "2.15% ... first VA loan with less than 5% down" and one exemption (disability compensation).
   - A repeat user under-budgets: on $300,000, 3.3% is $9,900 versus 2.15% at $6,450. Add the 3.3% and 1.5% / 1.25% tiers and the other exemptions the website already lists.
4. **checklist.js and pcs-checklist.html: "New York has three; you can claim one."**
   - Missing: they are local option, the March 1 filing date, a new P&T full exemption (Oct 1 2026), and that Cold War ended with Dec 26 1991 service, so most current members don't qualify for it.
5. **buying.html line 395:** "File Form RP-458-a with your assessor" gives no deadline. Add taxable status date (March 1 most towns, Jan 1 most villages). RP-458-a was revised 9/26 and now also covers P&T.
6. **pcs-checklist.html winter item, "natural gas recommended":** editorial, not VA. VA requires permanent heat holding 50°F where there is plumbing and names no fuel. The real rural risks are well, septic, roof and paint (Ch.12).
7. **buying.html line 220 and pcs-checklist line 248:** COE link and "lender can request it" are correct, but no documents are named, so Guard, Reserve and surviving-spouse families arrive unprepared (C2).

Checked and correct: the fee table in the FAQ, body and calculator ("since Apr 7, 2023"); the exemption list (Purple Heart, DIC, pre-discharge); "no PMI"; "no down payment"; RPTL 458-a percentages; the 10-year active-duty rule; school-district opt-in.

## (C) What our pages are missing, ranked by family impact
1. **How a VA appraisal treats older rural homes:** third-party well test (90 days, buyer can't collect it); signed acknowledgement for dug well, cistern, spring; pre-1978 peeling paint must be fixed; septic approval only if a problem is noted; repairs written "subject to" on the NOV; no WDI inspection required in the three counties unless damage is seen; get your own inspection.
2. **COE documents by buyer type:** DD214, statement of service, NGB-22 and NGB-23, retirement points statement, VA Forms 26-1817 and 21P-534EZ. The lender can pull the COE online.
3. **Re-use after a PCS:** 60-day occupancy; spouse or dependent child counts for active duty; deployment is fine; selling restores entitlement; one-time restoration; assumption.
4. **Full funding fee:** 3.3% second use, the complete exemption list, refund for a late rating, IRRRL at 0.5%.
5. **Costs:** 1% origination cap and allowable fees; 4% seller-concession cap and what doesn't count; only the funding fee can be financed; buyer-agent fees under Circular 26-24-14; NY attorney and CEMA fees are allowable.
6. **Underwriting honesty:** no VA minimum credit score but lender overlays; 41% DTI is a guideline; BAH and BAS count; Northeast residual income (family of 4, loan $80,000+: $1,025).
7. **NY closing costs:** mortgage recording tax (Jefferson $0.75 per $100, about $1,500 on $200,000; Lewis $1.00); attorney-led closing.
8. **Property types:** condo project approval, 2 to 4 units, manufactured home rules, new-construction appraisal, EEM.
9. **Low appraisal:** Tidewater (2 business days for comps) and ROV (5 business days).
10. **Program news:** VASP ended; VA Partial Claim replaces it (servicers ready by Nov 28 2026). Matters mostly after closing.
11. **Audience wording:** other pages say "soldiers" and "Soldiers report to...". Reword for every branch, Guard, Reserve and surviving spouses.
