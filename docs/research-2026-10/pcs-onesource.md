### 2. 2026 entitlements and what changed
- **DLA:** JTR change MAP 72-25(I), CY2026 rates, effective **Jan 1, 2026**, +3.8% (V-s, DTMO change list). Range $1,870.58 (E-1 no dependents) to $6,385.58 (top grade with dependents) matches secondary sources only (**U** for the exact table). Not eligible: below E-5 in single-type quarters; one per fiscal year. Advance: not guaranteed, see finance 10-15 days out, **80%** advanced (V, https://www.militaryonesource.mil/benefits/military-dislocation-allowance/ ).
- **TLE:** CONUS **21 days** (up from 14, effective Nov 27, 2024), **$290/day** cap per member, up to 60 days in approved housing-shortage areas in 10-day steps; no TLE and PCS per diem on the same day (V-s, https://www.travel.dod.mil/About/News/Article/Article/3981433/ and https://www.dfas.mil/MilitaryMembers/travelpay/armypcs/tle/ ). TLE is not for house hunting.
- **MALT:** **$0.235/mi** for PCS travel starting **July 1, 2026** (was $0.205), per authorized car, up to 2 cars (V-s, DTMO rate table and news article). Note DFAS MALT page still shows an old rate.
- **Per diem:** FY2027 standard CONUS, effective Oct 1, 2026: **$181 = $113 lodging + $68 M&IE** (FY2026 was $110 + $68) (V-s, https://www.travel.dod.mil/Travel-Transportation-Rates/Per-Diem/CONUS-Per-Diem-Rate-Changes/ ). PCS dependent percentages (75% / 50%): **U** (JTR not fetchable). MOS confirms 75% of M&IE per dependent only for the new HHG inconvenience payment.
- **PPM:** pays **100% of Government Constructed Cost** (the 130% summer-2025 bump is over per MOS March 2026 fact sheet; V-s). Advance up to **60%** (V-s). MOS FAQ: **45 days** to turn in paperwork (weight tickets, vehicle registration, receipts) (V). Army: first-time movers must counsel with the transportation office first.
- **Advance pay:** normally 1 month basic pay, up to 3 with commander approval, repaid over 12 months (V-s, Army/Navy/USMC counselor handouts). Form conflict (DA 4187 vs DD 2560): ask finance.
- **House hunting:** Army: up to 10 days permissive TDY, from losing or gaining commander, own cost, not if moving into government quarters immediately (V-s, AR 600-8-10 excerpt). Navy: 5 workdays, up to 10 calendar days (V-s). USMC: 10 days max (V-s). Air Force: **U**.
- **Pets:** up to **$550** per CONUS move, cats and dogs, itemized receipts, orders effective on/after Jan 1, 2024 (V-s, https://www.travel.dod.mil/Support/ALL-FAQs/Article/3624131/pet-transportation-allowance/ ).
- **Childcare after PCS:** pilot reimburses a temporary provider's travel, **$500 CONUS / $1,500 OCONUS**, only if no CDC care within 30 days of reporting; travel memo from MilitaryChildCare.com; pilot ends **Sep 30, 2027** (V-s, https://www.travel.dod.mil/Support/ALL-FAQs/Article/3944760/child-care-transportation-reimbursement/ ).
- **Travel voucher:** 5 working days after arrival (FMR Vol 9 Ch 5 Jan 2026; DTS regs) (V-s).
- **Weight allowance table / pro-gear:** MOS says see JTR; pro-gear 2,000 lb member, 500 lb spouse (V). Our rank table is **U** against the JTR table (could not open).

### 3. Household goods in 2026
- HomeSafe (Global Household Goods Contract) was terminated for cause in 2025. The **Personal Property Activity (PPA)** runs moves; legacy Tender of Service carriers; booking is via DPS with your local transportation office (V-s, https://www.war.gov/News/News-Stories/Article/Article/4256335/ ; https://www.ppa.mil/ ). Activation ceremony Scott AFB Sep 9, 2026 (V-s).
- PPA call center **833-MIL-MOVE (833-645-6683)**, Mon-Fri 8-5 CT; PCSCallCenter@mail.mil (V, Fort Drum page below).
- **Fort Drum PPSO**, Clark Hall Bldg P-10720, 2nd floor Rm A2-42, **315-772-6384**, Mon-Fri 7:30-4:00 (V, https://installations.militaryonesource.mil/military-installation/fort-drum/moving/household-goods ). Start when orders arrive.
- **move.mil is gone**: the domain no longer resolves (my fetch: ENOTFOUND). Content moved to militaryonesource.mil. DPS remains the booking system; MilMove is not mentioned on current MOS pages (U on MilMove status).
- **Claims:** written notice of loss/damage within **180 days** of delivery; itemized claim in DPS within **12 months** if pickup on/after **May 15, 2026** (9 months before); late = depreciated value only; MCO transfer within 2 years; carrier must acknowledge in 15 days, pay/deny/offer in 30 days (<=$1,000) or 60 days (>$1,000) (V-s, March 2026 fact sheet). Damage found after movers leave: alert the carrier in writing within **7 days** (V, MOS FAQ). New for May 15, 2026 pickups: late-delivery inconvenience payment, 75% of M&IE per dependent (V).
- Inventory: photos and receipts speed claims; movers do not carry hazmat; high-value items go with you (V).

### 4. Family programs
- **EFMP:** Fort Drum **315-772-5476** (V). Enrollment renewal every 3 years or on change; expired status can block orders; DD 2792 processing up to 4 weeks (V-s, Army sources). Screening happens before assignment (Army-sourced, other branches **U**).
- **Childcare:** CYS Parent Central Services **315-772-8675**, Clark Hall (V-s). MilitaryChildCare.com help desk 855-696-2934 (V). Fee assistance via Child Care Aware of America (**U** here; spouse page cites 800-424-2246).
- **Schools:** School Support Services / SLO **315-772-3214** and **315-772-0281**, Bldg 10720 Rm B1-17/B1-19, Mon-Fri 8:00-4:30 (V, https://drum.armymwr.com/programs/school-support-services/contact ). Official newcomer page: on-post families attend **Carthage CSD or Indian River CSD** (V). MIC3: covers Guard/Reserve children on active orders, not inactive; Space Force added 2023; medically retired covered 1 year (V-s, mic3.net).
- **Spouse jobs:** Fort Drum Employment Readiness **315-772-1090**, Bldg 10720 (V). License reimbursement up to **$1,000** (2018 NDAA); Army adds up to $1,000 business costs (Army Directive 2024-04) (V-s, myarmybenefits). Interstate law: SCRA amended by the Veterans Auto and Education Improvement Act (V, MOS). NY is **not** in the Nurse Licensure Compact (V-s, NYSED FAQ).
- **TRICARE:** Fort Drum is East Region, Humana Military **800-444-5445**, Mon-Fri 8-6 ET/CT (V-s). Keep your plan until you arrive, update DEERS in milConnect, **90 days** from address change to change plans, get referrals and refills before leaving, dental through United Concordia **844-653-4061** (V, https://www.tricare.mil/LifeEvents/Moving ). Clinic's current name is **Guthrie Army Health Clinic** (V-s, guthrie.tricare.mil).

### 5. Legal and paperwork
- **SCRA:** end a lease with PCS orders (or 90+ day deployment) by written notice plus copy of orders, hand-delivered or return-receipt mail; 6% cap on pre-service debts; phone and some car leases; covers Guard/Reserve on active orders (V, MOS). "Effective 30 days after next rent due" is statute but not printed on MOS (**U** here). DOJ 202-514-2000 (V).
- **MSRRA/SCRA residency:** spouse may keep the member's legal residence even if never lived there (2018 change) (V, MOS). NY nonresident spouse in NY only to be with member: NY wages exempt, file IT-203 code M2, IT-2104-E to employer (V, tax.ny.gov military page).
- **NY DMV:** new residents must get a NY license within **30 days** (V). Registration day count and inspection rule for military families: **U** (not on DMV military page). NY military pages: registration extended 60 days after return from service (V).
- **Voting:** new Federal Post Card Application each year and each address change; use Installation Voter Assistance office (V-s, fvap.gov).
- **Access:** Visitor Control Center Bldg 11008, **315-772-8585**, 5:30am-10pm daily; REAL ID needed; visitor sponsor request 72 hours ahead; Main Gate 24/7, Wheeler-Sack 24/7, Plummer 4:30am-10pm, Gasoline Alley closed (V, https://home.army.mil/drum/about/visitor-information ). DBIDS not mentioned on page (**U**).

### 7. Helpers
- **Army Emergency Relief:** zero-interest loans and grants; AER is Army-only. Real value to movers: HHG delay or TLE expiry loan.
- **Fort Drum Regional Liaison Organization:** now brands as **Advocate Drum** (since 2018); advocacy and community liaison, not a direct-service provider (secondary news only; **U** on current programs). Probably do not list as a mover helper.
- **Blue Star Families:** no Fort Drum chapter confirmed; NY Tri-State chapter exists (**U**). Hold until confirmed.
