### 3. New York benefits when moving or buying
- **Property tax (RPTL 458-a, 458-b, 458).** Source: Tax Dept Publication 1093 (9/24) and https://www.tax.ny.gov/pit/property/exemption/vetexempt.htm. V.
  - Forms: RP-458-a (Alternative), RP-458-b (Cold War), RP-458 (Eligible Funds). Change in disability rating: RP-458-a-Dis.
  - Deadline: the taxable status date, "in most communities" March 1. Confirm with the assessor.
  - **Every county, city, town, village and school district chooses whether to offer each exemption.** No special district levies.
  - Alternative: 15% (war) + 10% (combat zone) + half the service-connected rating, each capped by local choice. Statute caps (nysenate.gov): base $12,000/$8,000/$40,000 times equalization rate; local options range up to $45,000/$30,000/$150,000, or $75,000/$50,000/$250,000 in high-appreciation areas. Cap detail is from a nysenate.gov summary. U.
  - "Period of war" includes Persian Gulf (on or after Aug 2, 1990). Non-war service qualifies with an Armed Forces, Navy, Marine Corps or GWOT expeditionary medal, or Inherent Resolve Campaign Medal.
  - Reserve rule: still in the reserve component, released from active duty other than training, and the active duty was "significant and full-time".
  - Ownership: veteran, spouse, **unremarried surviving spouse**, or **Gold Star Parent if the community allows**. Primary residence only.
  - **Buying a new home:** file a new RP-458-a by the taxable status date.
  - Cold War (service Sept 2, 1945 to Dec 26, 1991): 10% or 15%, normally limited to 10 years. Eligible Funds: generally up to $7,500.
  - **New:** a municipal option for a full exemption for veterans VA rates permanently and totally disabled from service, for rolls with taxable status dates on or after Oct 1, 2026 (RPTL 458-a(11)). V (tax.ny.gov page).
  - Active duty: statute text defines "veteran" in the past tense. The "10 years plus reenlistment" language appears in a nysenate.gov summary only, not in the tax department's publication. U.
- **Veteran designation on license/permit/ID:** honorable discharge (or general under honorable conditions). Bring MV-44 plus proof (DD-214 etc.) to a DMV office, or mail photocopies. No extra fee. Not available online or by phone. V. https://dmv.ny.gov/more-info/veteran-status-designation-on-a-photo-document
- **Plates and registration:** custom military plates via CP-30. Fee-exempt registration of one vehicle for disabled veterans who got a Title 38 vehicle (MV-1E, VA Form 21-4502). V. https://dmv.ny.gov/more-info/information-for-military-and-veterans
- **Hunting and fishing (DEC):** Guard (Organized Militia) and U.S. Reserve residents get free fishing, hunting and trapping licenses (military ID plus Certificate of Active Military Affidavit). Veterans 40%+ service-connected get a $5 license plus free bowhunting/muzzleloading and Deer Management Permit preference (VA letter, once). A New York resident stationed in NY pays regular fees. V. https://dec.ny.gov/regulatory/permits-licenses/sporting-and-use/sporting/free-reduced-fee
- **Lifetime Liberty Pass:** NY resident veterans and Gold Star parents, spouses and children. Free entry to state parks and DEC day-use areas, discounted camping and cabins. Apply 518-474-2324. Fee and proof not stated. V. https://veterans.ny.gov/lifetime-liberty-pass
- **Civil service credit (Civ. Serv. Law 85):** disabled veterans (rated 10%+) 10 points on original appointment and 5 on promotion. Other veterans 5 and 2.5. Honorable discharge, citizen or permanent resident, NY resident. Claim before the list is established. V (nysenate.gov, official legislature site). https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/CVS/85
- **Veteran-owned business:** OGS Division of Service-Disabled Veterans' Business Development, 518-474-2015, Corning Tower, 32nd Floor, Empire State Plaza, Albany. 6% state contract goal. V. https://ogs.ny.gov/veterans
- **Education:** Veterans Tuition Award (VTA) covers up to SUNY resident undergraduate tuition. Four or more years of active duty, or combat service. Guard qualify with 4+ years deployed on active duty or combat. Open year-round. Not payable where Post-9/11 GI Bill or Yellow Ribbon covers tuition. V. https://hesc.ny.gov/vta
  - **MERIT** is not a general veteran benefit. It aids children, spouses and dependents of NY residents killed or severely and permanently disabled in service. V. https://veterans.ny.gov/state-education-scholarships
  - Regents Awards for Children of Deceased and Disabled Veterans: up to $450 a year (parent rated 40%+, POW, MIA or died). V.
- **Income tax:** military pension "totally exempt" for the retiree **or beneficiary**. Nonresident military pay is not taxed. Spouse relief is SCRA-based. V. https://www.tax.ny.gov/pit/file/military_page.htm
