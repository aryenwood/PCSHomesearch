- Current name is **North Country Spouses' Club** — formed in 2012 by merging the Officers' Spouses' Club and Enlisted Spouses' Club; open to spouses of all ranks and all genders.

## AREA 2 — CHILDCARE

### 1. Fort Drum CYS
- **Parent Central Services: Clark Hall, Bldg P-10720, 2nd floor — (315) 772-8675.** Registration required before any CYS care; enroll and request care via https://militarychildcare.com (create household profile, request care, join waitlists). Sources: https://home.army.mil/drum/about/Garrison/FMWR/CYS and https://drum.armymwr.com/programs/childandyouthservices
- **Four Child Development Centers** (ages 6 weeks–5 years; M–F 5:45am–5:45pm) — source https://drum.armymwr.com/programs/child-development-center-cdc:
  - Chapel Drive CDC — 10785C Chapel Drive, (315) 772-8676
  - Memorial Drive CDC — 10745 Memorial Drive, (315) 772-1085 — **currently closed until further notice** (per MWR page, Aug 2026)
  - Po Valley CDC — 4225 Po Valley Road, (315) 774-4831
  - South Riva Ridge CDC — 10780 South Riva Ridge Loop, (315) 772-1700
- Care formats: full-day, part-day, and hourly; hourly care at Chapel Drive CDC listed as closed until further notice (https://drum.armymwr.com/programs/parent-outreach-services/child-care-options).
- **School Age Center: Bldg 10795 Chapel Drive, (315) 772-4411**, M–F 5:45am–5:45pm; before/after-school for grades K–5 (before-school for grade 6 per garrison page), care on early-release days and breaks, 10–12 week summer camp; fees via WebTrac. Source: https://drum.armymwr.com/programs/school-age-centers
- **Family Child Care (FCC)**: certified home-based care on post, ages 4 weeks–12 years; providers background-checked, home-inspected, trained; info (315) 772-2250. Sources: https://drum.armymwr.com/programs/family-child-care and https://www.army.mil/article/257969/fcc_providers_open_their_homes_for_high_quality_child_care_at_fort_drum
- **CYS Babysitting Course**: completion plus parent application makes a certified babysitter on post; register online or at Parent Central; info (315) 772-6719. Source: https://drum.armymwr.com/calendar/event/babysitting-course/6039138/80658
- CYS admin office: 10799 N. Riva Ridge Loop (https://drum.armymwr.com/directory/24/child-and-youth-services)

### 2. Army Fee Assistance (off-post care)
- Administered for the Army by **Child Care Aware of America (CCAoA)**. Process: create household profile and request care on MilitaryChildCare.com → apply via ChildCareAware.org → complete Fee Assistance Enrollment Portal profile → accept offer on MCC. Provider must be state-licensed and CCAoA-enrolled (families can ask a Watertown provider to enroll).
- CCAoA: 800-424-2246, msp@usa.childcareaware.org
- Sources: https://www.childcareaware.org/fee-assistancerespite/military-families/army/afa-program/ ; https://www.armymwr.com/programs-and-services/cys/childcare-fee-assistance ; https://myarmybenefits.us.army.mil/Benefit-Library/Federal-Benefits/Army-Child-Care-Fee-Assistance-Programs?serv=122 ; https://www.militaryfamily.org/info-resources/child-care/how-to-apply-for-military-child-care-fee-assistance/
