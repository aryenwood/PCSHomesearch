# FORT DRUM SPOUSE RESOURCE RESEARCH (compiled August 2026)

## AREA 1 — SPOUSE EMPLOYMENT & EDUCATION

**MyCAA — renamed "SpouseWorks Scholarship" (major 2026 update)**
- Up to $4,000 total tuition assistance ($2,000/fiscal-year cap under the legacy structure) for licenses, certifications, associate degrees in portable fields, plus continuing-ed to maintain a credential.
- KEY CHANGE: Military OneSource now lists eligibility "across all ranks and pay grades" (active duty, Guard, reserve on Title 10) — expanded from the old E-1–E-9 / W-1–W-3 / O-1–O-3 caps. Source: https://www.militaryonesource.mil/benefits/mycaa-scholarship-program/ ; rename coverage: https://www.military.com/4000-mycaa-scholarships-renamed-spouseworks-heres-how-to-apply
- Portals: https://mycaa.militaryonesource.mil/mycaa/ and https://spouseworks.militaryonesource.mil/portal/getting-started ; phone 800-342-9647.

**Fort Drum ACS Employment Readiness Program (ERP)**
- Exact name: Employment Readiness Program (under Army Community Service). Resume help, interview prep, career planning, job lead referrals for spouses/family/DoD civilians/retirees.
- 10720 Mt Belvedere Blvd, Fort Drum, NY 13602; (315) 772-1090; Mon–Fri 8–4. Sources: https://drum.armymwr.com/programs/employment-readiness-program , https://drum.armymwr.com/employment-resources (note: domain is drum.armymwr.com, not fortdrum.armymwr.com)

**SECO / MSEP / remote work**
- SECO career coaching (free): 800-342-9647 — https://www.militaryonesource.mil/resources/gov/seco-and-msep-101/
- MSEP: 1,000+ partner employers, 360,000+ spouse hires since 2011 — https://msepjobs.militaryonesource.mil/msep/about-us
- FlexJobs: FREE 1-year subscription for military spouses via SECO (not merely a discount) — https://www.militaryonesource.mil/benefits/flexjobs/ , https://www.flexjobs.com/myseco
- Instant Teams (spouse-focused remote talent marketplace) — https://instantteams.com/military-spouse-remote-jobs/
- Hiring Our Heroes events calendar — https://www.hiringourheroes.org/career-services/hiring-events/

**Military Spouse Preference / federal jobs**
- MSP (EO 13473): priority consideration for DoD civilian jobs in the sponsor's commuting area; DoD phased out in-person PPP "Program S" registration — spouses now apply directly on USAJOBS to MSP-flagged postings. Sources: https://www.militaryonesource.mil/education-employment/for-spouses/understanding-military-spouse-preference/ , https://www.military.com/spouse/spouse-employment/heres-what-you-need-know-about-military-spouse-preference.html
- Noncompetitive military spouse appointing authority (any federal agency): https://www.opm.gov/fedshirevets/hiring-officials/strategic-recruitment-and-hiring/military-spouses-family-members/

**NY licensing for military spouses**
- NYSED expedited licensure (Ed Law §6501): expedited review, halved initial application fee, free temporary practice permit up to 6 months — https://www.op.nysed.gov/about/military-spouses-relocated-new-york-state-expedited-licensing-services ; 2023 temporary certification law: https://www.op.nysed.gov/temporary-certification-military-service-members-and-spouses-military-service-members
- **Nursing compact: NY is NOT an active NLC member as of Aug 2026.** Bills S3916/A4524 still in committee (https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/bills/2025/S3916 , https://legiscan.com/NY/bill/A04524/2025); nursinglicensure.org states "New York is not a member of the Nurse Licensure Compact" and multistate licenses can't be used in NY (https://www.nursinglicensure.org/state/nursing-license-new-york/). One 2026 industry guide (Prolink) claims "enacted/implementing" — CONFLICTING/UNVERIFIED. Safe copy: RN spouses must license by endorsement via NYSED, using the expedited spouse process.
- Teacher certification: expedited/fee-reduced via NYSED; local Fort Drum effort: https://www.army.mil/article/240620/local_effort_makes_teaching_certification_transfer_easier_for_military_spouses_in_new_york
- Cosmetology (NY Dept of State, not NYSED): reciprocity without exam from listed states; military household members can get reciprocity even if the home state doesn't reciprocate — https://dos.ny.gov/cosmetology-reciprocity
- DoD license reimbursement: ACTIVE — up to $1,000 per cross-state PCS for relicensing, plus up to $1,000 for business costs (Army Directive 2024-04, orders on/after Dec 21, 2023) — https://myarmybenefits.us.army.mil/Benefit-Library/Federal-Benefits/Reimbursement-of-Qualifying-Spouse-Relicensing-Costs-and-Business-Costs?serv=122 , https://www.militaryonesource.mil/benefits/transferring-your-professional-license/

**Local hiring events**: RecruitMilitary Job Fair at Fort Drum, Sept 24, 2026 — https://my.recruitmilitary.com/events/job-fair-at-fort-drum-september-24-2026 ; MWR career fair calendar — https://drum.armymwr.com/calendar/career-fairhiring-event
