# Fort Drum Area Things-To-Do Inventory (North Country NY)

Drive times are approximate from Fort Drum/Watertown and should be labeled "approx." on the site.

## 1. DAY TRIPS RANKED BY DRIVE TIME

### Under 30 minutes

**Thompson Park + New York State Zoo — Watertown (~20 min)**
- 355-acre Olmsted-designed historic park: golf, pool, tennis, playgrounds, hiking and cross-country ski trails. Source: https://www.watertown-ny.gov/ThompsonPark and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thompson_Park_(Watertown,_New_York)
- Zoo New York (opened 1920, 32 acres): exclusively New York native species — wolverines, black bears, eagles, mountain lions, wolves. Source: https://uncoveringnewyork.com/zoo-new-york-watertown/
- Easy 1.7-mile loop trail rated kid-friendly (paved/gravel, light hills, good for ages 2-4 per reviews). Source: https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/new-york/thompson-park-trails

**Sackets Harbor (~30 min)**
- Sackets Harbor Battlefield State Historic Site: War of 1812 battlefield, restored 1850s Navy Yard, Commandant's House, "Life on Board the Jefferson" exhibit, summer living-history reenactors in 1813 uniform, year-round interpretive trail, Lake Ontario picnic views. Sources: https://parks.ny.gov/visit/historic-sites/sackets-harbor-battlefield-state-historic-site and https://visit1000islands.com/listing/sackets-harbor-battlefield-state-historic-site/
- Village Bicentennial Trail footpaths connect to the battlefield for a flat ~6-mile walk. Source: https://www.battlefields.org/visit/itineraries/make-some-history-weekend-sackets-harbor
- Walkable Main Street with restaurants/breweries (see Section 3).

**Burrville Cider Mill — Watertown/Burrville (~20 min, fall)**
- 8+ decade institution: cider donuts, fresh-pressed cider, cheese curd, Croghan bologna; pressing Tue/Thu/Sat/Sun with self-guided tours at 10am and noon; open into November; has its own waterfall behind the mill. Sources: https://uncoveringnewyork.com/burrville-cider-mill-waterfall/ and https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g48812-d7363973-Reviews-Burrville_Cider_Mill-Watertown_New_York.html

**Black River Trail (~10-20 min)**
- Smooth, flat rail-trail along the river, ~4.5 miles ending in the village of Black River; ideal for strollers/bikes. Source: https://www.traillink.com (trail PDF) and https://hmienterprises.com/nnytrails/

### 30–60 minutes

**Clayton (~40 min)**
- Antique Boat Museum: premier freshwater boating museum; steam vessels commissioned by Singer Castle's Frederick Bourne; hosts America's oldest annual antique boat show + auction. Sources: https://www.singercastle.com/partnerships/ and https://www.1000islands-clayton.com/tours/
- Clayton Island Tours runs castle trips from Clayton (315-686-4820). Source: https://www.claytonislandtours.com via https://www.singercastle.com/visitor-information/
- Walkable riverfront downtown; Wood Boat Brewery (Section 3).

**Alexandria Bay (~45 min)**
- Boldt Castle on Heart Island: boat-access only; open mid-May to mid-Oct, grounds 10:30am–6:30pm; admission ~$10 adult / $7 child (5-12); Power House and Alster Tower (playhouse with bowling alley). Sources: https://www.boldtcastle.com/visitorinfo/visitor-information and https://www.boldtcastle.com/visitorinfo/hours-of-operation
- Uncle Sam Boat Tours (since 1926): Two-Nation Tour, Millionaires Row, Boldt/Singer Castle stopovers, lunch/dinner/sunset cruises; 315-482-2611. Source: https://www.usboattours.com/
- Bill Johnston's Pirate Days each August (Section 4).

**Singer Castle on Dark Island (~1 hr to Hammond/Chippewa Bay launches)**
- 45-min guided tour of 4 floors (Great Hall, library, hidden passages era rooms, 4th-floor dormitory); adult $14.75, child $7.75, **military rate $12.75 adult / $6.75 child**; shuttle from Schermerhorn Harbor (Wed–Sat in summer 2026, 315-324-5966); also reachable via Uncle Sam Boat Tours and Clayton Island Tours. Source: https://www.singercastle.com/visitor-information/

**Cape Vincent (~50 min)**
- Tibbetts Point Lighthouse (1827): where Lake Ontario meets the St. Lawrence; oldest lighthouse in Jefferson County, only original working Fresnel lens on Lake Ontario; grounds open daylight hours. Sources: https://www.tibbettspointlighthouse.org/ and https://www.iloveny.com/listing/tibbetts-point-lighthouse/3632/
- Village: farmers market, DEC Research Station aquarium display, ferry to Wolfe Island (Canada), historic district. Source: https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g47412-d105680-Reviews-Tibbett_s_Point_Lighthouse-Cape_Vincent_New_York.html

**Wellesley Island State Park (~50 min)**
- Largest camping complex in the 1000 Islands; beach; Minna Anthony Common Nature Center with live animals and miles of trails (hike/snowshoe/XC ski). Source: https://www.iloveny.com/blog/post/48-hours-in-alexandria-bay/

**Beaches** — Westcott Beach and Southwick Beach: see Section 2.

**Whetstone Gulf State Park — Lowville (~55 min)**: see Section 2.

### 1–2 hours

**Kingston, Ontario (~1.5 hr via Thousand Islands Bridge)**
- Fort Henry National Historic Site: 19th-century fort with costumed interpreters, open daily 10am–5pm in season. Sources: https://www.visitkingston.ca/stories/a-family-friendly-kingston-itinerary/ and https://beavertravelers.com/canadas-oldest-military-fortification-exploring-fort-henry-with-kids/
- Marine Museum of the Great Lakes, Miller Museum of Geology (AR sandbox, dino bones), Military Communications and Electronics Museum (escape rooms), Lake Ontario Park playground/splash pad. Source: https://koa.com/campgrounds/kingston/blog/kid-friendly-things-to-do-in-kingston-on_d557c2c8-e398-48ca-a7a8-b33a6576cd2f/
- **Crossing notes:** cross at the Thousand Islands Bridge (Lansdowne Port of Entry, toll both ways); passport, passport card, or NEXUS required; no visa under 180 days. Sources: https://www.visitkingston.ca/faqs/ and https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/international-travel/International-Travel-Country-Information-Pages/Canada.html — MILITARY NOTE (UNVERIFIED specifics): soldiers should confirm current leave/pass policy for international travel with their chain of command; Fort Drum has an on-post Official Passport and Visa Center (https://home.army.mil/drum/about/Garrison/directorate-human-resources/MPD/personnel-processing-branch-ppb/passports). Kingston is reachable on a weekend pass per https://www.vetrafi.com/articles/5-things-you-should-do-your-next-4-day-weekend-fort-drum-edition

**Salmon River Falls Unique Area — Orwell, Oswego County (~1 hr 10 min)**
- 110-ft waterfall; easy 0.2-mile Falls Trail with overlook platforms (kid-friendly); demanding 600-ft Gorge Trail to the riverbed; free, limited parking, no restrooms. Sources: https://dec.ny.gov/places/salmon-river-falls-unique-area and https://nyfalls.com/waterfalls/salmon-river-falls/

**Syracuse (~1 hr 15 min)**
- Rosamond Gifford Zoo at Burnet Park: 700+ animals (Asian elephants, Amur tigers, penguins); 2.5–3 hrs with young kids. Source: https://www.visitsyracuse.com/blog/post/the-top-kid-friendly-activities-in-syracuse-new-york/
- MOST (Milton J. Rubenstein Museum of Science & Technology), Armory Square: hands-on science, planetarium, only domed IMAX in NY State; best ages 4–12. Source: https://www.visitsyracuse.com/blog/post/exploring-syracuse-a-guide-to-family-fun/
- Destiny USA megamall: rainy-day indoor backup, ~15 min from the zoo. Source: https://www.syracusegrand.com/blog/kid-friendly-syracuse-weekend.html

**Old Forge, Adirondacks (~1.5–2 hr)**
- Enchanted Forest Water Safari: NY's largest water theme park, 50+ rides including 32 heated water rides. Source: https://www.watersafari.com/old-forge-thrills/ and https://www.iloveny.com/listing/enchanted-forest-water-safari/1950/
- Calypso's Cove (go-karts, bumper boats, zip-line, arcade), McCauley Mountain Scenic Chairlift (late June–early Oct), Old Forge Lake Cruises 2-hr Clearwater sightseeing voyage. Source: https://www.experienceoldforge.com/things-to-do/water-safari-resort/

### Worth a weekend

**Ottawa, Ontario (~2.5 hr)**
- Rideau Canal Skateway (winter): world's largest naturally frozen rink (UNESCO site, ~5 miles, free; skate/sled rentals on-site). Source: https://ottawatourism.ca/en/see-and-do/what-to-do-during-winterlude
- Winterlude festival (Jan 30–Feb 16, 2026): ice sculptures, snow playground, mostly free. Source: https://ottawatourism.ca/en/see-and-do/what-to-do-during-winterlude
- Free Parliament tours (West Block House of Commons + Senate), Canadian Museum of Nature, Canadian Museum of History, ByWard Market. Sources: https://ottawatourism.ca/en/see-and-do/family-activities-ottawa and https://blog.trekaroo.com/top-10-things-to-do-in-ottawa/
- Montreal is ~3.5 hr from Fort Drum for a bigger weekend. Source: https://www.vetrafi.com/articles/5-things-you-should-do-your-next-4-day-weekend-fort-drum-edition

## 2. OUTDOORS BY SEASON

### Hiking (spring–fall)
- **Thompson Park Trails, Watertown** — 1.7-mi loop, easy, kid-friendly, paved/gravel. https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/new-york/thompson-park-trails
- **Black River Trail** — flat, smooth, stroller/bike friendly, 4.5 mi. https://hmienterprises.com/nnytrails/
- **Whetstone Gulf State Park, Lowville** — 3-mile gorge on the Tug Hill edge; North/South Rim gorge loop 5.6 mi moderate with waterfall side-trip; NOT for unsupervised small kids (cliff edges); also camping (56 sites), man-made swimming area, Whetstone Reservoir canoeing/fishing (tiger muskie, largemouth). https://parks.ny.gov/visit/state-parks/whetstone-gulf-state-park and https://cnyhiking.com/NYSP-WhetstoneGulf.htm
- **Inman Gulf / Rainbow Falls, Tug Hill State Forest (Barnes Corners area)** — 6.9-mi moderate loop over a 200–300-ft-deep canyon; caution near edges. https://tughilltomorrowlandtrust.org/explore/rainbow-falls/ and https://cnyhiking.com/TugHillStateForest.htm
- **Gleasmans Falls, Independence River Wild Forest (Watson, Lewis Co.)** — 50-ft falls, 6.6-mi round trip, mostly flat but rated difficult for length. https://digthefalls.com/gleasmans-falls-lowville/
- **Salmon River Falls** — easiest big-payoff waterfall (0.2-mi trail, kid-friendly overlooks). https://dec.ny.gov/places/salmon-river-falls-unique-area
- **Robert G. Wehle State Park, Henderson** — Lake Ontario shoreline bluffs, multi-use trails (hike/MTB/XC ski/snowshoe), famous sunsets. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_G._Wehle_State_Park and https://visit1000islands.com/stories/chasing-sunsets/
- **Minna Anthony Common Nature Center, Wellesley Island** — live animal exhibits + easy family trails. https://www.iloveny.com/blog/post/48-hours-in-alexandria-bay/
- On-post: Fort Drum Trails and James R. Kanik Nature Trail (per AllTrails: https://www.alltrails.com/us/new-york/fort-drum).

### Beaches (summer)
- **Westcott Beach State Park, Henderson** — ~20 min from Watertown, closest real beach; $7/car; lifeguarded swimming; picnic tables. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westcott_Beach_State_Park and https://discovernorthcountry.com/10-beaches-near-watertown-new-york/
- **Southwick Beach State Park, Henderson** — 3,500-ft sandy beach, among the best on Lake Ontario, ~40 min; $8/car May–Oct; guarded swimming June 19–Sept 6, 2026. https://parks.ny.gov/visit/state-parks/southwick-beach-state-park
- **On-post: Remington Park beach** on Remington Pond (Section 5).

### Fishing (year-round)
- **Henderson Harbor / Lake Ontario "Golden Crescent"** — 60+ charters; lake trout, king salmon, brown trout, steelhead, smallmouth; season roughly April–Sept. https://www.visithendersonharbor.com/fishing-charters.html and https://fishingbooker.com/destinations/location/us/NY/henderson-harbor
- **Black River** — bass/pike water 10 min from post, plus salmon/steelhead runs in fall; charters from Black River from ~$120. https://fishingbooker.com/destinations/location/us/NY/black-river
- **Salmon River (Pulaski, ~50 min)** — most famous Lake Ontario tributary; 15–40-lb king salmon (Sept–Oct), steelhead (fall–spring). https://lotsalimits.com/salmon-river-fishing/ and https://www.good-times-sportfishing.com/
- Named charters: Moby Dick Charters (https://mobydickcharters.com/), Lots A Limits (https://lotsalimits.com/), Good Times Sportfishing (https://www.good-times-sportfishing.com/), Excursion Charters (http://excursionfishingcharters.com/)
- Ice fishing gear rentable from MWR Outdoor Rec (Section 5).

### Winter (the Tug Hill snow machine — 200+ inches)
- **Dry Hill Ski Area, Watertown** — 10 min south of downtown; 1 lift, 7 trails, 300-ft vertical, terrain park + snow tubing park with snowmaking; the learn-to-ski hill; known for military discounts. https://www.skidryhill.com/ and https://nyskiblog.com/directory/ny-ski-areas/tug-hill/dry-hill/ and https://mybaseguide.com/things-to-do-near-fort-drum-ny
- **Snow Ridge, Turin (~50 min)** — 230" average snowfall; 22 trails, 6 lifts, night skiing, glades, tubing, backcountry acreage. https://snowridge.com/ and https://www.iskiny.com/snow-ridge-resort
- **Maple Ridge Snow Park, Lowville** — 8+ tubing lanes 1,200 ft long, 100-ft vertical, tube tow, Black River Valley views; weekends/school holidays 12–5; natural snow only. CAUTION: operator transitions reported (BOCES ended operation) — verify current status before publishing. https://mapleridgecenter.org/ and https://www.nny360.com/news/lewiscounty/boces-looking-for-options-to-continue-snow-tubing-at-maple-ridge/article_f77656b4-dd28-5a00-aea0-4a52d76ad724.html — free XC skis on the connected fairgrounds trail network per https://mapleridgecenter.org/
- **Snowmobiling** — Tug Hill is the Northeast's snowmobile capital (UNVERIFIED superlative); club-groomed networks: Osceola Snowmobile Club, Penn Mountain Snow Riders, Turin Ridge Riders, Valley Snow Travelers of Lewis County; trail info at https://tughill.org/links-local/ and https://www.visittughill.com/
- **Sledding locals use** — Thompson Park's "famous hill" (documented by local press photo essays: https://www.nny360.com/news/jeffersoncounty/photos-sledding-at-thompson-park/article_90b1e91e-1cfd-11ea-bfc8-bf79b392c046.html); Dry Hill tubing for a lift-served version.
- XC skiing/snowshoeing: Whetstone Gulf, Wellesley Island, Wehle State Park trails (sources above).

## 4. ANNUAL EVENTS CALENDAR (month-by-month)

- **January/February — Snowtown USA, Watertown** (Thompson Park): ice skating at the rotary pavilion, family winter festival; name coined from Walter Cronkite's Blizzard of '77 coverage. https://snowtownusa.org/ — **Ottawa Winterlude** Jan 30–Feb 16, 2026. https://ottawatourism.ca/en/see-and-do/what-to-do-during-winterlude
- **March — NYS Maple Weekend** (two weekends): sugarhouse open houses across Lewis/Jefferson counties; International Maple Museum Centre in Croghan runs pancake breakfasts ($12 adult/$7 child). https://www.mapleweekend.com/producers/ and https://maplemuseumcentre.org/
- **May — Boldt Castle season opens** (mid-May). https://www.boldtcastle.com/visitorinfo/hours-of-operation
- **June — beaches open; Singer Castle shuttle season starts** (June 17, 2026). https://www.singercastle.com/visitor-information/
- **July — Cape Vincent French Festival** (July 10-11, 2026; 56th year): French pastries, parade, fireworks, crafters village. https://capevincent.org/frenchfestival/ — **Jefferson County Fair, Alex Duffy Fairgrounds, Watertown** (209th fair, July 2026; longest continuously operating fair in the USA, since 1817). https://www.jeffcofair.org/ — **Can-Am Festival, Sackets Harbor**: parade, fireworks, vintage baseball, soapbox derby celebrating US-Canada friendship. https://seawayregion.com/festivals/ — **Fort Drum Mountainfest**: on-post one-day festival, public welcome, Salute to the Troops concert + fireworks. https://mybaseguide.com/things-to-do-near-fort-drum-ny (exact 2026 dates for Can-Am and Mountainfest: UNVERIFIED)
- **August — Antique Boat Show & Auction, Clayton** (first weekend of August; America's oldest antique boat show). https://topeventsusanews.blogspot.com/2011/07/august-events-in-1000-islands-region.html — **Bill Johnston's Pirate Days, Alexandria Bay** (second Saturday to third Sunday of August, ~10 days): pirate invasions, live entertainment. https://visitalexbay.org/our-events/bill-johnstons-pirates-days-2026/
- **September/October — salmon run on the Salmon River; Burrville Cider Mill peak foliage season** (open into November). https://lotsalimits.com/salmon-river-fishing/ and https://uncoveringnewyork.com/burrville-cider-mill-waterfall/
- **November–March — Watertown Wolves hockey** (FPHL) at Watertown Municipal Arena, Duffy Fairgrounds — family-priced pro hockey with frequent military appreciation nights. https://www.watertownwolves.net/ and https://mybaseguide.com/things-to-do-near-fort-drum-ny — summer counterpart: **Watertown Rapids** collegiate baseball. https://watertownrapids.baseballshift.com/
- **December — Watertown Christmas Parade** (early Dec, Washington St to City Hall, free hot chocolate from the Downtown Business Association). https://www.nny360.com (via WWNY: https://www.wwnytv.com) — Clayton/1000 Islands holiday events: UNVERIFIED specifics.

## 5. ON-POST MWR RECREATION

All at https://drum.armymwr.com:
- **Remington Park** (Bldg 11115 Iraqi Freedom Dr, 315-772-8222; open May–Oct): on Remington Pond — beach, 5 large pavilions plus medium pavilions, playgrounds, volleyball, horseshoes, fishing docks (catch-and-release), fitness trail, cabin/RV site rentals. https://drum.armymwr.com/programs/remington-park
- **Outdoor Recreation / "Gear to Go" equipment rental**: winter — ice-fishing gear, ski/snowboard packages, snowshoes, XC skis, fat-tire bikes; summer — boats, campers, canoes, tents, bikes, grills, yard games. Rental day = pickup to noon next business day; half-cost deposit to reserve. Also houses the recreational shooting complex, firearm/vehicle storage, hiking club, and Warrior Adventure Quest. https://drum.armymwr.com/programs/outdoor-recreation
- **Leisure Travel Services (LTS/ITR)** (same building; Mon–Fri 8:00–2:30; 315-772-8222 / 772-5169): discounted tickets — examples on the current price list: Disney World 4–6 day Park Hoppers $409–$479, Disneyland $314–$505, Universal Orlando $215–$255, Legoland NY from $49.50, Dollywood, Nickelodeon Universe. https://drum.armymwr.com/programs/leisure-travel-services-lts/tickets-and-pricing (local-attraction tickets like Water Safari/Dry Hill through LTS: UNVERIFIED — call for current list)
- **Magrath Sports Complex**: three full-size basketball courts, two racquetball courts, 178m indoor track, free weights to 160-lb dumbbells, Life Fitness machines, cardio rooms; also home of the Intramural Sports Office (leagues/tournaments). https://drum.armymwr.com/programs/fitness-centers/magrath
- **Automotive Skills Center**: bays, tools, and instruction for DIY vehicle maintenance at low cost for soldiers, families, DoD civilians. https://home.army.mil/drum/about/Garrison/FMWR (via https://drum.armymwr.com/directory)
- **10th Mountain Division & Fort Drum Museum** — division history, free. https://visit1000islands.com/listing/10th-mountain-division-fort-drum-museum/
