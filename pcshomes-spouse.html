Start the day you get orders — not the day you arrive
Register with CYS Parent Central ServicesClark Hall, Bldg P-10720, 2nd floor · (315) 772-8675. Registration is required before any CYS care — CDCs, School Age Center, home-based care, sports, SKIES classes, all of it.
Create your household profile at MilitaryChildCare.comGo to militarychildcare.com, request care, and join waitlists there. Waitlists move on need dates, so the sooner you're in the system the better.
Going off post? Apply for Army Fee AssistanceThe Army offsets off-post care costs through Child Care Aware of America: request care on MilitaryChildCare.com, apply at childcareaware.org, complete the Fee Assistance Enrollment Portal profile, then accept your offer. Your provider must be state-licensed and CCAoA-enrolled — you can ask a Watertown provider to enroll. CCAoA: 800-424-2246.

Your career doesn't stall here

The dollar-denominated programs nobody briefs you on
SpouseWorks (formerly MyCAA) — up to $4,000Tuition assistance for licenses, certifications, and associate degrees in portable fields, plus continuing ed to maintain a credential. Military OneSource now lists eligibility across all ranks and pay grades (active duty, Guard, reserve on Title 10). Start at the SpouseWorks portal or call 800-342-9647.
Relicensure reimbursement — $1,000 + $1,000When a PCS crosses state lines (orders on/after Dec 21, 2023), the Army reimburses up to $1,000 in relicensing/certification costs plus up to $1,000 in qualifying business costs. Details at Military OneSource.
NY expedited licensing for military spousesNYSED gives spouses expedited application review, a halved initial application fee, and a free temporary practice permit for up to 6 months while your license transfers. Teachers get expedited, fee-reduced certification through NYSED too. Start here.
Remote work with real backingMSEP connects you to 1,000+ partner employers committed to hiring military spouses (msepjobs.militaryonesource.mil). SECO gives spouses a free one-year FlexJobs subscription — not a discount, free (details). Instant Teams is a spouse-focused remote talent marketplace.
Military Spouse Preference for federal jobsMSP gives you priority consideration for DoD civilian jobs in the commuting area. In-person "Program S" registration is phased out — apply directly on USAJOBS to postings flagged for military spouse preference.
Mark the calendar: RecruitMilitary Job FairAt Fort Drum, September 24, 2026. Register free, and watch the MWR career fair calendar for more.
The big gotcha for nurses
New York is NOT in the Nurse Licensure Compact.

As of August 2026, NY is not an active NLC member — your multistate license cannot be used to practice here. RN spouses must apply for licensure by endorsement through NYSED, using the expedited military-spouse process above. Start the paperwork the day you get orders, and use the 6-month temporary permit to keep working while it processes.

Mom logistics

Doctors, delivery, urgent care, and getting groceries without three car seats
Know before you need it
There is no hospital on Fort Drum.

The Guthrie Ambulatory Health Care Clinic (11050 Mount Belvedere Blvd, appointments (315) 772-2778) is outpatient-only. ER and inpatient care happen at civilian hospitals, and families are commonly assigned civilian network PCMs. Your TRICARE East contractor is Humana Military — confirm any provider is in network via the Humana Military directory.

Pregnant? Start hereFort Drum OB/GYN triage line (315) 785-4609. Delivery is at Samaritan Medical Center's Car-Freshner Center for Women and Children — 8 LDR rooms, C-section suite, about 1,400 births a year, the area's only Level II NICU (12 beds), and a dedicated Sherman St maternity entrance.
